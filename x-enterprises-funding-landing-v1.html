§ 01 The pattern

Three ways this goes wrong, and they all start the same way.

None of these are unusual. They are what happens when the rules are only explained after the decision, which is to say almost always.

Personal cards become the business

Inventory, payroll gaps, equipment, ad spend. It all lands on personal credit at consumer interest rates. The balance follows you personally, and every dollar of it counts against the same profile you will later need for the business.

Declines compound quietly

An unprepared application produces an inquiry and a decline. Several of them in a short window makes the next approval harder than the first one would have been. The damage is cumulative and invisible until you look.

The business has no file at all

An entity with no EIN-linked history, no dedicated bank account, and nothing reported is invisible to underwriting. There is nothing to read, so everything gets judged on you personally instead.

§ 02 The instruments

Three instruments. Three different bars to clear.

Most owners only ever hear about the first one. Which fits depends on what the money is for, how fast it comes back, and what documentation you can produce. These are alternatives, not a ladder, and picking the wrong one is how affordable money turns expensive.

Instrument Underwritten on Fits The catch
Business credit cards Light documentation Your personal profile, almost entirely. Needs an entity and EIN, but revenue history matters far less here than anywhere else. Short-cycle spend that recycles quickly. Inventory, ad spend, equipment. Introductory rate periods are why owners start here. What the rate becomes when the introductory period ends, and the personal guarantee behind it.
Business line of credit Full financial file The business itself. Bank statements, tax returns, time in business, and the consistency of your deposits. Working capital and cash-flow gaps you draw against and repay. Usually cheaper than carrying a card balance. Documentation is the whole barrier. If the records do not exist, neither does the line.
Personal-backed No business file needed You. Personal profile and personal income, deployed into the business. Starting capital when the business has no history of its own and no realistic alternative yet. The obligation is yours, and it consumes personal capacity you will want later. Worth choosing deliberately, not landing on.
§ 03 The factors

Six factors set your limits.

Ranked by how quickly you can actually move each one, because that is the only ordering that changes what you do this month. The top of this list is where a few weeks of work shows up.

  1. 01

    Credit utilization

    How much of your existing revolving credit is drawn. It is judged on the day your file is pulled, not on your plans for next month.

    Weeks
  2. 02

    Recent inquiries

    How many applications you have submitted lately and how they were spaced. Sequence and timing are things you control completely.

    Immediate
  3. 03

    Entity and EIN setup

    Registered entity, EIN, business bank account, and details that match across every record. Mismatches read as risk.

    Days to weeks
  4. 04

    Documentation on hand

    Statements, returns, formation papers. Not a scoring factor exactly, but the most common reason a file stalls mid-review.

    Days
  5. 05

    Personal credit profile

    Score band, payment history, derogatory marks. Carries the most weight of anything here, and moves the slowest.

    Months
  6. 06

    Time in business and revenue

    How long the entity has operated and how consistent its deposits are. This gates which instruments are open to you at all.

    Not on demand

§ 05 Fit

Worth reading, or worth skipping.

Read it if

  • ✓You own a registered business, or you are about to register one.
  • ✓You have a specific use for the capital and a plan to repay it.
  • ✓Your personal credit is workable, or you are willing to work on it.
  • ✓You would rather understand the process than be sold on it.

  • ✗You need money this week for something already past due.

§ 07 Questions

Before you download.

Is this a loan from X Enterprises?

No. X Enterprises is a funding consultant. We help you prepare the file, understand the options, and apply in a sensible order. Any credit extended comes from third-party banks and issuers, on their terms and at their discretion.

Does downloading this affect my credit?

No. A checklist is not an application. Nothing on this page involves a credit pull of any kind.

Can you guarantee approval, or tell me my number?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Approvals and limits are set by the issuer based on your file at the moment you apply. What we can do is make sure you understand what is being read and that the file is in the best shape it can be first.

What does working with you cost?

It depends which path fits, so we cover it on the call rather than posting a number that would not apply to you. You get the fee structure in plain terms before you are asked to decide anything.

What if my credit is not good right now?

Then this is the most useful thing you will read this week, because it shows which factors to address and in what order. To be clear about what we are not: we are not a credit repair organization and we do not dispute items on your report.
