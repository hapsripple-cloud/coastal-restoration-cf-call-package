The first workflow

Emergency intake to urgency classification to dispatch-ready packet.

Intake

Capture loss type, caller type, property area, payment path, photos, access notes, and route.

Classification

Separate emergency triage, same-day callback, estimate request, referral handoff, and office queue.

Packet

Send a concise summary with next action, route, and open questions for the person responding.
