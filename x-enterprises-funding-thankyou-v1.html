What it is

A working conversation about your file. If we are a fit to help you execute it, we will say so and tell you what that costs. If you are better off handling it yourself, we will say that instead. It happens often and it costs you nothing to hear.

What it is not

Not an application. Nothing on the call pulls your credit. We will not quote you an approval amount, because nobody can do that honestly before an issuer has reviewed your file.
