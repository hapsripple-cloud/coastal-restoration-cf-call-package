# Coastal Restoration x Stone Gate AI

Prepared for Coastal Restoration of Central Florida

## What Stone Gate AI Builds

Stone Gate AI automates the workflows that decide whether a service business makes money. For Coastal, the first rollout should focus on the front-end operating layer: intake, dispatch, documentation, and follow-up.

## What We Are Solving First

- Calls are answered, but the handoff is not always restoration-ready.
- Urgent calls, estimates, and referral handoffs need clearer routing.
- Documentation needs consistent photos, readings, scans, and job-file completeness.
- Water/storm opportunity makes triage and dispatch readiness more important.
- Referral partners should be captured and tracked cleanly.
- The website is currently returning a WordPress database/server error, which is a quick credibility repair.

## Recommended Path: Growth

Recommended package: Growth at $5,000/month, with the separate setup fee waived for this initial start.

Growth is the right fit because Coastal's issue is not isolated. Intake affects dispatch, dispatch affects documentation, documentation affects follow-up, and referral tracking affects the next best opportunity. Growth connects the handoff instead of fixing one piece in isolation.

Growth includes 3 workflow categories:

1. Sales / Lead Intake.
2. Operations / Dispatch.
3. Service Delivery / Documentation.

The first 5 workflows installed first:

1. Emergency restoration intake + job packet.
2. On-call routing / dispatch handoff.
3. Estimate and missed-opportunity follow-up.
4. Documentation SOP checklist.
5. Referral partner intake / tracking.

During onboarding, the system is measured against Coastal's actual calls, jobs, and follow-up activity.
